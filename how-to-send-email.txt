TRAVYN EMAIL NOTIFICATION SETUP

Why mail was not sending:
A normal HTML/CSS/JS website opened with index.html cannot send email automatically.
Browsers block direct email sending for security. So this fixed version includes a Node.js backend.

How to run:
1. Install Node.js.
2. Open this folder.
3. Rename .env.example to .env.
4. Add your SMTP email password/app password.
5. Double click START-WEBSITE.bat
6. Open http://localhost:3000
7. Login admin and send notification.

Gmail setup:
Use Gmail App Password, not your normal Gmail password.
Google Account > Security > 2-Step Verification > App Passwords.

WhatsApp:
WhatsApp still opens chat links because WhatsApp bulk automatic sending requires WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
